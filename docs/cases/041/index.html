Case No.041

Kevin Finisterre / DJI Bug Bounty

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2017.0
Chilling effect

Finisterre found DJI's wildcard SSL certificate and AWS keys exposed on GitHub, accessing customer flight logs, passports, and IDs. After reporting through DJI's bug bounty, DJI allegedly threatened CFAA charges and required a restrictive NDA before paying the $30,000 bounty. Finisterre declined and published the dispute publicly.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Can bug bounty confidentiality terms be used to suppress legitimate findings?; (ii) Threatening prosecution against a researcher acting within a company's disclosed scope; (iii) Power imbalance between corporate legal teams and individual researchers
