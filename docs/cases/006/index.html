Case No.006

University of Minnesota (UMN)

Informed Consent & Human Subjects · 2021.0
Chilling effect

UMN researchers submitted deliberately buggy/malicious patches to the Linux kernel without consent to test maintainer review processes. Linux banned UMN from future contributions and reverted all prior commits.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Experimenting on open-source community without consent; (ii) Misuse of volunteer maintainer time; (iii) Risk of introducing real vulnerabilities into critical infrastructure; (iv) Publication of methods enabling similar attacks

Source
