Case No.014

MedSec Holdings / Muddy Waters (St. Jude Medical)

Financial Conflict of Interest · 2016.0
Chilling effect

MedSec found serious vulnerabilities in St. Jude Medical cardiac implant devices. Rather than disclosing to the vendor, they partnered with investment firm Muddy Waters to short St. Jude's stock and released partial vulnerability details publicly.

Corporate / private sector
Financially motivated (profit, stock, bounty)
Media-first
Civil suit / Injunction

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Financial conflict of interest in vulnerability disclosure; (ii) Potential patient risk from partial/premature disclosure; (iii) Bypassing coordinated disclosure entirely for profit; (iv) Whether short-selling on security research is legal/ethical
