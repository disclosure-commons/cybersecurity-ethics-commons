Case No.014

Hacking Team Data Breach / Leak Ethics

Dual-Use Research · 2015

An unidentified hacker or hacking group compromised the Italian surveillance company Hacking Team and publicly released approximately 400 GB of internal data, including correspondence, customer information, source code, and exploits. The leak exposed the company's business relationships with governments accused of human rights abuses and created additional security risks by exposing attack tools.

Journalist / activist / independent
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Media-first
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Research ethics of using stolen/leaked corporate data; (ii) Whether exposing surveillance vendors justifies privacy violations of their employees; (iii) Researchers re-releasing zero-day exploits found in leaked data; (iv) Corporate accountability for selling offensive tools to authoritarian states
