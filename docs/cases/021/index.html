Case No.021

J. Alex Halderman / Georgia Dominion Voting Machines

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2017–2024
Chilling effect

University of Michigan professor Halderman was given court-supervised access to Georgia's Dominion voting machines and developed proof-of-concept attacks. His sealed report was later partially unsealed and seized upon by election-denial conspiracy theorists.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Academic freedom vs. potential to fuel election misinformation; (ii) Whether sealed vulnerability research can remain secret in an adversarial legal setting; (iii) Risk that legitimate security findings are weaponized for political disinformation; (iv) Tension between public right to know and protecting public trust in elections
