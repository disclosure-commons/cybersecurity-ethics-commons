Case No.124

Thomas Roth / German Telecommunications Firm

Dual-Use Research · 2011
Chilling effect

Roth built a password-cracking tool running on Amazon EC2. His apartment was raided, his bank account frozen, and he was kept from releasing the tool at Black Hat DC; the injunction was later revoked and he published the research.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil injunction (later lifted)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Releasing a cheap, cloud-based cracking tool: defensive awareness or attacker uplift? (ii) Are raids and frozen accounts proportionate responses to a planned talk? (iii) Should dual-use tools be released openly or to vetted users only?
