Case No.015

J. Alex Halderman / Georgia Dominion Voting Machines

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2020–2024
Chilling effect

University of Michigan researcher J. Alex Halderman examined Georgia's Dominion ImageCast X ballot-marking devices under court-authorized access and found vulnerabilities that could allow attackers to manipulate recorded votes under certain conditions. His findings raised concerns about election security, vulnerability remediation, and the potential misuse of security research to promote unfounded election fraud claims.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Academic freedom vs. potential to fuel election misinformation; (ii) Whether sealed vulnerability research can remain secret in an adversarial legal setting; (iii) Risk that legitimate security findings are weaponized for political disinformation; (iv) Tension between public right to know and protecting public trust in elections
