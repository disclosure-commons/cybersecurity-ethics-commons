Case No.015

Aaron Swartz / MIT / JSTOR

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2011
Chilling effect

Aaron Swartz downloaded millions of academic articles from JSTOR using MIT's network, reportedly intending to make research more widely accessible. Although JSTOR settled its civil dispute, federal prosecutors continued criminal charges under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act and other statutes. Swartz died by suicide in January 2013 before trial.

Journalist / activist / independent
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
Charged (case ended with death of defendant)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) CFAA overcriminalization — was exceeding ToS really a felony?; (ii) Prosecutorial overreach when the victim (JSTOR) dropped the case; (iii) Open-access ideology vs. intellectual property law; (iv) Criminal penalties disproportionate to actual harm
