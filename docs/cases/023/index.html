Case No.023

Justin Shafer / Patterson Dental / FBI Raids

Institutional Suppression · 2016–2018
Chilling effect

Texas dental IT researcher Shafer found patient health data on a publicly accessible anonymous FTP server and reported it responsibly. Patterson Dental complained to the FBI; Shafer's home was raided three times. He spent 8 months in pretrial detention on cyberstalking charges (for Facebook posts), ultimately pleading to one misdemeanor.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Plea to one misdemeanor

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) 'Shoot the messenger' — vendor weaponizing CFAA against the person who exposed their negligence; (ii) Using cyberstalking statutes to silence researcher criticism of government overreach; (iii) Chilling effect: other researchers stopped disclosing after the raids; (iv) Pretrial detention as punishment before conviction
