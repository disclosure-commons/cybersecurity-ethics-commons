Case No.085

Coalfire / Iowa Judicial Branch & Dallas County Sheriff

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2019–2020
Chilling effect

The Iowa state court administration hired Coalfire to test the physical security of its courthouses. During an authorized after-hours test, the Dallas County sheriff arrested employees Gary DeMercurio and Justin Wynn and charged them with burglary, disputing the state's authority over a county building. Charges were dropped on January 30, 2020, four months later.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Authorized engagement (contracted test)
Charged / dropped

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Whose permission counts when the client authorizes a test but another authority controls the premises? (ii) When scope is ambiguous mid-engagement, should testers stop or proceed on the client's written word? (iii) Does publicizing the arrest protect future testers, or make clients less willing to authorize physical testing?
