Case No.092

Raphaël Rigo (Airbus Security Lab) / Xerox

Institutional Suppression · 2021
Chilling effect

Rigo's Infiltrate 2021 talk on vulnerabilities in Xerox multifunction printers was cancelled an hour before it was due, citing pending legal action. He gave the talk on April 22, 2021.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil threat (talk delayed)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should vendors be able to use last-minute legal threats to delay talks on already-reported flaws? (ii) What do conferences owe speakers facing legal pressure? (iii) When a vendor objects, does delaying a talk serve users or only the vendor?
