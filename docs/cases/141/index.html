Case No.141

SNOsoft / Hewlett-Packard (Tru64)

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2002

HP invoked the DMCA in threatening SNOsoft over a Tru64 Unix vulnerability. The two later agreed on a disclosure timeline, and additional Tru64 vulnerabilities were published.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil threat (DMCA)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should copyright law apply to publishing an operating system vulnerability? (ii) Is a negotiated timeline after a threat a fair outcome? (iii) Did using the DMCA here set a precedent that helped or hurt the field?
