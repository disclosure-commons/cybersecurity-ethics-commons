Case No.163

EncroChat encrypted-phone investigation

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2020

EncroChat sold modified phones with encrypted messaging, marketed as private French and Dutch authorities got into the network and installed malware on the devices, reportedly in 2020 they collected messages from tens of thousands of users, which led to thousands of arrests across Europe the data was shared with other countries’ police defendants challenged the evidence, arguing it was intercepted illegally courts in several countries mostly allowed the evidence, but debate continued the French operation’s technical details stayed secret

Government / law enforcement
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
Thousands of arrests; legal challenges

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is it acceptable to hack every user of a service if most are suspected criminals? (ii) Should defendants be able to challenge evidence when the technique stays secret? (iii) What happens to legitimate users caught up in the operation?
