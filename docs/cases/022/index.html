Case No.022

Citizen Lab / FinFisher / State Surveillance

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2012–2018

Citizen Lab (University of Toronto) repeatedly identified and exposed governments using FinFisher spyware to surveil dissidents and journalists in Bahrain, Ethiopia, and Turkey, potentially tipping off targeted governments to improve their tradecraft.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Does publishing detailed attribution of surveillance operations tip off abusive governments?; (ii) Researcher responsibility to notify both victims and software vendors; (iii) Balance between academic publication norms and protecting at-risk human rights defenders; (iv) Whether Western companies should face consequences for selling spyware to authoritarian buyers
