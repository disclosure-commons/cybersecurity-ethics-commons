Case No.022

Ola Bini / Ecuador / Privacy Tool Developer

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2019
Chilling effect

Swedish software developer and digital privacy advocate Ola Bini was arrested in Ecuador in 2019 and accused of unauthorized access to computer systems. Authorities and prosecutors treated elements of his technical expertise and privacy-related activities as suspicious. Following years of disputed proceedings, a 2023 acquittal, and a subsequent appellate conviction, he was deported to Sweden in October 2026 under disputed national security allegations.

Journalist / activist / independent
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
Charged (politically motivated; international support campaign)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Criminalization of possession of security tools and technical literature as evidence of malicious intent; (ii) Politically motivated prosecution of security researchers; (iii) Due process rights of open-source developers; (iv) Chilling effect on privacy-tool development
