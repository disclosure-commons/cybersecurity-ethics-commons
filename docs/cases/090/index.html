Case No.090

Aaron Toponce / what3words (WhatFreeWords)

Institutional Suppression · 2019–2021
Chilling effect

what3words, which sells its location-addressing system to emergency services, threatened the WhatFreeWords open-source reimplementation in 2019. In April 2021 Toponce received a legal letter demanding he stop sharing the software after researchers had analyzed how the wordlist could produce confusable addresses.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Proprietary algorithms used by emergency services versus the public's interest in independent analysis of how they fail. (ii) Is sharing a reimplementation of a company's method legitimate research or IP infringement? (iii) How should researchers weigh legal risk when the safety stakes are public?
