Case No.128

Scott Jarkoff (TechMiso) / RSA (Navy Federal Credit Union)

Institutional Suppression · 2009

Jarkoff's site published a report on security issues with Navy Federal Credit Union's website. RSA challenged the publication, and RSA backed down after TechMiso and its host pushed back.

Journalist / activist / independent
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Civil threat (withdrawn)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should a security vendor act to suppress reporting about a client's website? (ii) Is publishing first justified when a financial institution's members are exposed? (iii) What role should hosting providers play when pressured to remove research?
