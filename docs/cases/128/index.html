Case No.128

Vipsta & MurderSkillz / SimpleBlog (8pixel.net)

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2006

Researchers using the names Vipsta and MurderSkillz reported SQL injection flaws in 8pixel.net's SimpleBlog 2.3, an ASP-based blogging application. An incident archive reports that the vendor threatened legal action soon after notification; the researchers then published proof-of-concept material. The linked exploit alone does not establish the reported legal threat.

Independent / contractor
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Media-first
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is releasing an exploit two days after a legal threat retaliation or protection of users? (ii) Should a threat end a researcher's duty to coordinate? (iii) How should small open-source theme authors handle reports they don't understand?
