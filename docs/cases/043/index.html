Case No.043

Flavio Garcia / University of Birmingham (VW & Thales)

Institutional Suppression · 2012–2015
Chilling effect

Garcia and Dutch colleagues cracked the Megamos Crypto algorithm used in vehicle immobilizers across 26 car brands. Volkswagen and Thales obtained a UK High Court injunction blocking publication for ~2 years; it was lifted after researchers agreed to redact a single sentence.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Civil injunction (later lifted)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should a manufacturer be able to suppress academic publication of a flaw in its own product?; (ii) Reverse engineering legitimately purchased hardware vs. 'breach of confidence'; (iii) Public's right to know about car-theft vulnerabilities vs. risk of enabling theft
