Case No.077

Dennis Giese and Braelynn Luedtke / Digilock

Institutional Suppression · 2024
Chilling effect

Before DEF CON 2024, security researchers Dennis Giese and Braelynn Luedtke received a cease-and-desist demand from electronic-lock maker Digilock over their planned presentation on vulnerabilities in keyless locks. The talk was postponed briefly while lawyers reviewed the dispute, then proceeded. This was a lock-security disclosure dispute, not a robot-vacuum dispute.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Cease-and-desist dispute; presentation proceeded

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Corporate retaliation; (ii) Responsible disclosure; (iii) Research freedom
