Case No.125

Hari Prasad / ECIL Electronic Voting Machines (India)

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2010
Chilling effect

Prasad co-authored a 2010 paper showing vulnerabilities in India's electronic voting machines. Mumbai police arrested him and pressed him to reveal the anonymous source who provided the machine; he spent seven days in custody before release on bail. disclose.io also lists the case among incidents that crossed the line because the machine was allegedly stolen.

Independent / contractor
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Research publication
Arrested (released on bail)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is research on an improperly obtained voting machine justified by the public interest in election integrity? (ii) Should researchers protect an anonymous source at personal legal risk? (iii) Does government pressure on election-security research undermine or protect public trust?
