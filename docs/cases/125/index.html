Case No.125

Sid Karunaratne / BeThere (Be Unlimited)

Institutional Suppression · 2007
Chilling effect

Karunaratne published information about a backdoor in routers supplied by his ISP, Be Unlimited. The ISP terminated his service; passwords were redacted, a patch was supplied, and his service had not been restored as of July 2007.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Service terminated (no charges)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is publishing details of an ISP router backdoor justified when every customer is exposed? (ii) Is cutting off a customer's service a legitimate response or retaliation? (iii) Should passwords have been redacted before publication, not after?
