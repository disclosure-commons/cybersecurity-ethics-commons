Case No.058

Dmitry Sklyarov / ElcomSoft / Adobe DMCA

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2001.0
Chilling effect

Russian ElcomSoft employee Sklyarov was arrested by the FBI immediately after his DEF CON talk on breaking Adobe eBook encryption — the first criminal charge under the DMCA. Held in custody then under travel restriction before a deal requiring his testimony against ElcomSoft in exchange for dropped charges.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Charged (charges dropped in exchange for testimony)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should conduct legal in a researcher's home country (Russia) trigger US criminal arrest at a US conference?; (ii) Conference attendance as a venue for law enforcement action against speakers; (iii) Using one defendant's testimony as leverage to convict their employer
