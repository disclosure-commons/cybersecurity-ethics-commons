Case No.058

Verkada Camera Breach / Tillie Kottmann / FTC

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2021

In 2021, hackers accessed Verkada's cloud-camera infrastructure, exposing live feeds and other sensitive information from customer sites. The incident was attributed to hacker Tillie Kottmann, who faced separate federal charges relating to hacking activity. In 2024, the FTC required security improvements and a $2.95 million civil penalty for unrelated commercial-email violations; that dollar amount was not a fine for the camera breach.

Independent / contractor
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Media-first
Federal hacking indictment; FTC security order and separate CAN-SPAM penalty

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is breaching a surveillance company to expose its reach a form of protected political/security activism or straightforward criminal hacking?; (ii) Does the target being a surveillance company change the ethical calculus of an unauthorized breach?; (iii) What accountability should cloud security camera vendors face when their poor security exposes sensitive environments like hospitals and prisons?; (iv) When a vendor's negligence enables mass surveillance exposure, who bears moral responsibility — the hacker or the vendor?
