Case No.120

Trevor Eckhart / Carrier IQ

Institutional Suppression · 2011

Eckhart documented that Carrier IQ software installed on phones logged excessive user information. Carrier IQ sent him a cease-and-desist letter, then retracted it after EFF involvement and negative attention. The research stayed public.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Civil threat (retracted)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Public interest in exposing hidden phone tracking versus a vendor's claim of confidentiality. (ii) Should privacy findings about software users can't remove go straight to the public? (iii) What changed the outcome: the law, or public pressure?
