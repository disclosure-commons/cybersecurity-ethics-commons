Case No.120

Lance James / Comerica Bank

Institutional Suppression · 2009
Chilling effect

James published XSS and phishing vulnerabilities on Comerica Bank's website. Comerica sent a cease-and-desist to Tumblr, which removed the information while the vulnerability remained present.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Civil threat (post removed)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Removing the report while the flaw stays live: who does that protect? (ii) Should platforms honor takedowns of security research about unpatched flaws? (iii) Was public-first disclosure appropriate for a phishing-enabling bank flaw?
