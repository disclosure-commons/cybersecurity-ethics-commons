Case No.056

Hendrik H. / Modern Solution GmbH (Germany)

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2021–2025
Chilling effect

While fixing a client's software, an IT contractor found a hardcoded plaintext database password in an executable, used it to confirm the scope of exposure (nearly 700,000 customer records), and reported it. Modern Solution reported him to police instead. He was ultimately convicted of unlawful data access and fined €3,000.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Convicted (€3,000 fine, upheld on appeal)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should using a password the vendor itself left in plaintext constitute criminal 'unauthorized access'?; (ii) Criminalizing the verification step required to confirm a vulnerability is real; (iii) Disparity between vendor's negligence (unpunished) and researcher's penalty (criminal record)
