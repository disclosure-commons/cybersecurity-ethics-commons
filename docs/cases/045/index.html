Case No.045

Eric McCarty / University of Southern California

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2005–2006
Chilling effect

McCarty exploited a SQL injection flaw in USC's online applicant database, downloading seven records from a database containing 275,000+ applicants' SSNs, before anonymously reporting it. He pleaded guilty to CFAA charges; sentenced to probation and $36,800 restitution.

Independent / contractor
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Responsible/coordinated
Convicted (probation, restitution)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Does proving a vulnerability by exploiting it cross from research into a prosecutable crime?; (ii) Proportionality of felony record/restitution vs. alerting the university; (iii) Effect of 'bragging' online on prosecutorial framing of intent
