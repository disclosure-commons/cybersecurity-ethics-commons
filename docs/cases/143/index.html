Case No.143

Michal J. / Wowza Media Systems

Institutional Suppression · 2013

A researcher reported authentication vulnerabilities in Wowza's media server; the vendor threatened to 'reevaluate' his independent consultant status, among other indirect threats. He disclosed after the vendor refused a remediation plan and left its consultant program. disclose.io lists this as unconfirmed.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Vendor non-responsive → public disclosure
Financial pressure (consultant status)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) When a researcher also earns income from the vendor, can disclosure decisions stay independent? (ii) Is threatening someone's business relationship a legal threat by another name? (iii) Should a vendor's refusal to share a remediation plan trigger disclosure?
