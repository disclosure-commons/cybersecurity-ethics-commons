Case No.028

iSight Partners / Sandworm Zero-Day

Financial Conflict of Interest · 2014

iSight Partners discovered Russian group Sandworm exploiting a Windows zero-day (CVE-2014-4114) to target NATO and Ukrainian government systems. iSight had used the intelligence for weeks before coordinating a patch release with Microsoft, monetizing it as a client intelligence product.

Corporate / private sector
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Private threat-intelligence firms monetizing zero-day knowledge vs. immediate public disclosure; (ii) Whether commercial security sector should have a legal disclosure obligation; (iii) Conflict between client confidentiality and warning undefended third parties; (iv) Government contractors holding offensive capabilities creates asymmetric vulnerability landscape
