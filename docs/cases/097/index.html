Case No.097

Unnamed 18-year-old / BKK & T-Systems (Budapest e-ticket)

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2017
Chilling effect

An 18-year-old reported flaws in Budapest's new e-ticket system, including plain-text password emails, an IDOR exposing other users' data, ticket cloning, price manipulation and an admin password of 'adminadmin.' He was arrested; BKK claimed his report went to the wrong address, which he disputed with a screenshot. The arrest drew thousands of one-star reviews and a 500-person protest.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Arrested (further action unclear)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) How can a researcher prove a price-manipulation flaw without committing fare fraud? (ii) When a vendor disputes receiving a report, who bears the burden of proof? (iii) Does public outrage protect researchers, or harden institutions against them?
