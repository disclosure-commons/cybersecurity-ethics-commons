Case No.106

Mike Davis (IOActive) / CyberLock

Institutional Suppression · 2015

A law firm representing CyberLock threatened a DMCA-based lawsuit over Davis's research into cloning CyberKey high-security keys. A day after the threat became public, IOActive published the advisory.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Civil threat (DMCA)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should anti-circumvention law cover research into physical security products? (ii) Publishing the day after a public threat: principled stand or escalation? (iii) What do owners of high-security locks need to know, and when?
