Case No.151

Dillon Beresford & Brian Meixell (NSS Labs) / Siemens & DHS

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2011

Beresford and Brian Meixell pulled their TakeDownCon 2011 talk on Siemens PLC vulnerabilities after Siemens and DHS's ICS-CERT raised concerns; Siemens had found at the last minute that its planned mitigation did not work. Both researchers and NSS Labs said they were asked, not forced. Beresford presented the research at Black Hat in August 2011.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Talk withdrawn (vendor/government concern)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should government concern about critical infrastructure override a researcher's publication plan? (ii) Is voluntary withdrawal after an official request different from censorship? (iii) When a vendor's fix fails at the last minute, who should decide whether the talk goes ahead?
