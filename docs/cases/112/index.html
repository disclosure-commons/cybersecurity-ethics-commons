Case No.112

William 'AmmonRa' Turner / Environment Canterbury Metrocard

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2013
Chilling effect

Turner reported flaws in Christchurch's Metrocard bus fare system to Environment Canterbury; three months later nothing had been fixed, and he disclosed them at Kiwicon 7. ECan called the police, and claims followed that about 70,000 free rides had been taken.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Vendor non-responsive → public disclosure
Police complaint (no charges reported)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) After three months of inaction, was a public talk the right lever? (ii) If disclosure led to widespread fare evasion, who bears responsibility: the researcher or the operator who didn't fix it? (iii) How much technical detail is appropriate for a transit-fare flaw?
