Case No.114

Acidgen (Corelan) / Magix AG

Institutional Suppression · 2011
Chilling effect

Researchers reported a buffer overflow in Magix Music Maker 16 and were threatened under Germany's anti-hacking law. They published anyway and persuaded Magix to change its stance; Magix opened a researcher portal whose terms bar disclosure before a fix.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is a vendor portal that forbids disclosure until a fix exists a step forward or a gag? (ii) Should researchers accept programs whose terms remove their timeline? (iii) Did publishing under threat help future researchers or only this case?
