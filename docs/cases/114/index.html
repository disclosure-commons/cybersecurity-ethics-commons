Case No.114

'Patrick' / Zamfoo

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2013

After two weeks without a patch, a researcher told Zamfoo he would post a proof of concept if the fix didn't come faster. Zamfoo threatened to sue him.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Vendor non-responsive → public disclosure
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) A two-week deadline with a public proof of concept: fair pressure or an ultimatum? (ii) How short can a disclosure deadline be before it becomes coercion? (iii) Does threatening a PoC change how a vendor will read the researcher's intent?
