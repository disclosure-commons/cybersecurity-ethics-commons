Case No.032

Jason Parker / Georgia Voter Cancellation Portal

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2024.0

Independent researcher Jason Parker found a critical flaw in Georgia's online voter-cancellation portal: anyone knowing a voter's name, birth date, and county could cancel any voter's registration. He notified the Secretary of State's office; the office denied receiving the notification. ProPublica published the story; the flaw was quickly patched.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) When a government office denies receiving researcher notification, what recourse does the researcher have?; (ii) Timing of media disclosure for critical election infrastructure vulnerabilities; (iii) Risk that responsible disclosure of election bugs becomes fodder for disinformation; (iv) Whether election officials' denial of researcher contact constitutes bad faith
