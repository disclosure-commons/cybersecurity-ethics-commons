Case No.127

Wayne Huang (Armorize) / Taiwanese Government

Institutional Suppression · 2010
Chilling effect

Huang's Black Hat 2010 talk, an archaeological study of China's government-backed hacking from 2001 to 2010, was cancelled two weeks before the conference due to pressure from the Taiwanese government.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Talk cancelled (government pressure)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) When geopolitical concerns conflict with research publication, whose interests should prevail? (ii) Should researchers comply with pressure from a government that isn't their direct regulator? (iii) Does suppressing threat-actor research help or harm defenders?
