Case No.024

Aaron Swartz / MIT / JSTOR

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2011–2013
Chilling effect

Swartz bulk-downloaded ~4.8M academic articles from JSTOR using MIT's network, apparently intending to release them freely. JSTOR settled civilly and dropped charges; federal prosecutors charged him with 13 CFAA felonies (up to 50 years). Swartz died by suicide two days after rejecting a plea deal.

Journalist / activist / independent
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
Charged (case ended with death of defendant)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) CFAA overcriminalization — was exceeding ToS really a felony?; (ii) Prosecutorial overreach when the victim (JSTOR) dropped the case; (iii) Open-access ideology vs. intellectual property law; (iv) Criminal penalties disproportionate to actual harm
