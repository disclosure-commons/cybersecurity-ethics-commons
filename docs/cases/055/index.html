Case No.055

Wesley Wineberg / Facebook / Instagram Bug Bounty

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2015.0
Chilling effect

Through Facebook's bug bounty, Wineberg found an RCE flaw on an Instagram server, then continued probing and obtained SSL/signing keys and access to Amazon storage buckets. Facebook's CSO contacted Wineberg's employer to push back; Facebook denied threatening legal action but said further exfiltration was unauthorized.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed (employer pressure applied)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Where does legitimate 'proof of impact' end and unauthorized data exfiltration begin within a bug bounty's scope?; (ii) Going around a researcher to pressure their employer instead of addressing the researcher directly; (iii) Ambiguity in bug bounty terms about how far a researcher may go to demonstrate severity
