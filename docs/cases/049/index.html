Case No.049

Sakura Samurai / UN & Indian Government

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2021.0

Volunteer research group Sakura Samurai targeted organizations with published VDPs, finding exposed credentials giving access to 100,000+ UN employee records and credentials across 26 Indian government departments. They limited actions to verification and disclosed through official channels; no charges were filed.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Does scanning specifically because an org has a VDP make later 'out of scope' findings authorized?; (ii) Should past black-hat convictions disqualify someone from later legitimate research?; (iii) Reliance on government agencies to respond competently once notified
