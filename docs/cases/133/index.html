Case No.133

Halvar Flake / U.S. Customs

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2007
Chilling effect

Halvar Flake was denied entry into the United States on his way to teach security training, and the training was cancelled at the last minute.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Denied entry (no charges)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should border authorities be able to block researchers from sharing defensive knowledge? (ii) How should international researchers weigh travel risk when presenting in other countries? (iii) Do conferences owe foreign speakers legal and logistical support?
