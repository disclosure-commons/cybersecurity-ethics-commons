Case No.154

EncroChat encrypted-phone investigation

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2020

In 2020, French and Dutch authorities compromised the EncroChat encrypted-phone network used by many organized-crime groups and obtained large volumes of messages for criminal investigations. The operation led to thousands of arrests, while defendants in several countries challenged the legality and admissibility of the collected evidence.

Government / law enforcement
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
Thousands of arrests; legal challenges

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is it acceptable to hack every user of a service if most are suspected criminals? (ii) Should defendants be able to challenge evidence when the technique stays secret? (iii) What happens to legitimate users caught up in the operation?
