Case No.149

Sergey Gordeychik & Denis Baranov (Positive Technologies) / SCADA

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2012

The researchers' DEF CON 20 talk, 'SCADA Strangelove: How I Learned To Start Worrying And Love The Nuclear Plants,' promised about 20 new vulnerabilities in SCADA systems including Siemens SIMATIC WinCC. They pulled it about a week before the conference and it was replaced with another talk; no reason was given, and reporters speculated about vendor pressure. They later presented SCADA Strangelove research at 29C3 in December 2012.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Talk cancelled (reason unconfirmed)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) How much industrial-control vulnerability detail, and what tools, are safe to release at a hacker conference? (ii) How should the community interpret a cancellation with no stated reason? (iii) Should ICS findings go through national CERTs and vendors before any public talk?
