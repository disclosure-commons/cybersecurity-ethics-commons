Case No.075

WinPot ATM Jackpotting Malware (Academic Study)

Dual-Use Research · ~2023

Academic researchers published a holistic study of WinPot ATM jackpotting malware — a strain used by criminal groups to force ATMs to dispense cash — analyzing its attack vectors, exploitation techniques, and defensive countermeasures in detail. The paper provided comprehensive technical documentation of how ATM jackpotting works, raising dual-use concerns that the level of technical detail could serve as an implementation guide for criminal actors even as it aimed to advance defensive knowledge.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) How much technical detail is appropriate in academic publications about criminal malware when the same detail that helps defenders also helps attackers?; (ii) Should researchers publishing analyses of criminal tools be required to coordinate with affected vendors (ATM manufacturers, banks) before publication?; (iii) Is studying and publishing criminal malware ethically different from studying and publishing proof-of-concept researcher-written exploits?; (iv) What responsibility do academic publishers and program committees bear for the dual-use risk of papers they accept?
