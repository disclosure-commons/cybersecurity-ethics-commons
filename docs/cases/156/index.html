Case No.156

Operation Trojan Shield / ANOM

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2021

the FBI, working with Australian police, secretly ran an encrypted-phone company called ANOM the phones were marketed to criminal groups through underworld distributors every message was quietly copied to law enforcement the operation ran for about three years and was announced in June 2021 led to hundreds of arrests across many countries the FBI got access to messages outside the US through a third country’s cooperation, which was a legal workaround

Government / law enforcement
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
Law-enforcement operation; mass arrests

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is it acceptable for police to run a deceptive “secure” product? (ii) Should agencies be able to route around domestic legal limits through foreign partners? (iii) Does this make people less willing to trust real privacy tools?
