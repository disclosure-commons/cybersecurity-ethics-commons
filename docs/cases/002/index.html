Case No.002

Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) / MBTA

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2008.0
Chilling effect

Three MIT students found vulnerabilities in the MBTA subway ticketing system as a class project and planned to present at DEF CON. The MBTA sued under the CFAA and obtained a gag order; it was later lifted.

Student
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Civil suit / Injunction

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Research freedom vs. legal liability; (ii) Using litigation to suppress legitimate security findings; (iii) Free speech rights of security researchers; (iv) Responsible disclosure timeline
