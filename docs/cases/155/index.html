Case No.155

Operation Rubicon / Crypto AG

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2020

Crypto AG was a Swiss company selling encryption devices to governments around the world it was secretly owned by the CIA and West German intelligence (BND) devices were rigged so the agencies could read customers’ “secure” communications customers thought they were buying neutral, trustworthy equipment exposed in 2020 through a Washington Post / ZDF / SRF investigation of a leaked CIA history reporting says the intel was valuable, but agencies also saw abuses by some customer governments and did not act

Government / intelligence
Malicious / reckless (knowingly harmful)
None (withheld)
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is deceiving customers acceptable for national security? (ii) Should intelligence agencies act on what they learn about human rights abuses, or protect the source? (iii) Does a case like this damage trust in all encryption vendors?
