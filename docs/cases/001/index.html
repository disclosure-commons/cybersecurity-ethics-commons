Case No.001

Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT)

Dual-Use Research · ~2018
Chilling effect

- what is tor: the open-source privacy network that hides your IP address and online activity - Used deep learning tactics to create "deep fingerprinting" to find patterns in web traffic - Seems like a research project: multiple students and a professor - They created their own line of defense against their attack (adding noise) but then they looked into ways to attack their own defense (packet timing information) - Tor was notified about this and were supportive research partners

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Where did the training data come from, and did it include other people's browsing traffic?; (ii) Publishing a working attack that can identify Tor users vs. helping Tor build defenses; (iii) How much technical detail to release about a deanonymization method; (iv) Is it responsible to attack your own proposed defense, and who should see those results first?
