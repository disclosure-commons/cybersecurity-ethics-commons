Case No.001

Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT)

Dual-Use Research · ~2018
Chilling effect

A researcher accessed the dark web to collect zero-day vulnerability data, encountering illegal content, hacker tools, and a fund-stealing script.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Ethics of handling illegal data incidentally encountered; (ii) How to store/destroy sensitive research data; (iii) IRB gap for dark-web research; (iv) Obligation to report illegal content to authorities

Source
