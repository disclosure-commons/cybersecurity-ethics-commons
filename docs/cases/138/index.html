Case No.138

NGSSoftware / Sybase

Institutional Suppression · 2005

Sybase threatened NGSSoftware over planned publication of database vulnerabilities. The threat was dropped and the research published.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Civil threat (dropped)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should database customers learn about flaws even when the vendor objects to timing? (ii) Do license terms limit what researchers can say about benchmarked or tested software? (iii) Does a dropped threat still chill other researchers?
