Case No.126

Varun Uppal & Gyan Chawdhary / Financial Industry Client

Financial Conflict of Interest · 2010
Chilling effect

A talk on hacking high-speed trading systems was pulled from a security conference under financial pressure, the potential loss of a client, and was never presented elsewhere.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Talk withdrawn (client pressure)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should a firm's client relationships decide whether research is published? (ii) Do markets benefit more from silence or from disclosure of trading-system flaws? (iii) Who represents the public interest when the only objector is a paying customer?
