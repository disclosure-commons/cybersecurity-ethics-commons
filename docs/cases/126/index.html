Case No.126

Chris Paget (IOActive) / HID Global

Institutional Suppression · 2007
Chilling effect

Paget's Black Hat DC talk on RFID security problems in HID access cards was pulled after a legal threat from HID, and the research was not published.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil threat (talk pulled)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should a vendor be able to stop research on access-control cards protecting buildings? (ii) Does withholding RFID cloning research protect card holders or leave them unaware? (iii) Should an employer pull a talk to avoid litigation costs?
