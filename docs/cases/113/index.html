Case No.113

Brenno de Winter / Trans Link Systems (OV-chipkaart)

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2011
Chilling effect

Journalist Brenno de Winter demonstrated weaknesses in the Dutch OV-chipkaart transit payment systemby manipulating card balances and traveling with modified cards. He was questioned by police after a complaint by the system's operator, Trans Link Systems. However, the prosecutors subsequently decided not to pursue criminal charges. The dispute raised questions about reporting security flaws in public services.

Journalist / activist / independent
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Criminal complaint (questioned)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should journalists demonstrate a transit-card flaw themselves to prove a story? (ii) Is prosecuting a reporter a proportionate response to fare fraud risk? (iii) Where is the line between investigative journalism and exploitation?
