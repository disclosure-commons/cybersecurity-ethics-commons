Case No.095

Chris Kubecka / Boeing

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2019
Chilling effect

Kubecka reported finding serious weaknesses in Boeing's network, including internet-exposed test environments, malware infections and XSS flaws, and spoke publicly about them. Boeing, which had no disclosure policy, allegedly threatened legal action to stop her speaking and considered damaging her reputation.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) When a company with no disclosure policy ignores safety-relevant findings, is a public talk justified? (ii) How much detail about an aviation manufacturer's network weaknesses is safe to share publicly? (iii) Does a researcher's status (veteran, well-known speaker) change how the vendor should respond?
