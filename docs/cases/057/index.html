Case No.057

Randal L. Schwartz / Intel Corporation

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 1993–2007
Chilling effect

Former Intel contractor Schwartz ran a password-cracking tool against Intel's systems, saying he wanted to demonstrate that security had deteriorated since his departure. Convicted of three Oregon felony counts; sentenced to probation, 480 hours community service, and $68,000 restitution. Conviction set aside and expunged in 2007.

Independent / contractor
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
None (withheld)
Convicted (later expunged)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should 'improving security' justify unauthorized access against a former employer's systems?; (ii) Multi-decade reputational and financial cost of a later-overturned felony conviction; (iii) Employer's choice to pursue criminal prosecution rather than civil/internal remedies
