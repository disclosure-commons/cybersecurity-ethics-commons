Case No.107

Raphaël Rigo / Blue Coat

Institutional Suppression · 2015
Chilling effect

Rigo cancelled his SyScan 2015 talk on Blue Coat ProxySG after pressure from Blue Coat, passed on through the conference founder. He later presented the research at Ruxcon and Black Hat Europe.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Talk cancelled (later presented)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) When pressure comes through a conference organizer rather than a lawyer, is it still a threat? (ii) Should an employer's relationship with a vendor decide when its researcher speaks? (iii) Did the delay protect anyone, or only postpone the inevitable?
