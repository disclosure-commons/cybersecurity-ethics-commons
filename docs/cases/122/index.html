Case No.122

Brenno de Winter / Trans Link Systems (OV-chipkaart)

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2011
Chilling effect

De Winter, a journalist, reported on flaws in the Dutch OV-chipkaart transit payment system. A criminal complaint followed and he was questioned; the government took legal action against him for covering the issue.

Journalist / activist / independent
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Criminal complaint (questioned)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should journalists demonstrate a transit-card flaw themselves to prove a story? (ii) Is prosecuting a reporter a proportionate response to fare fraud risk? (iii) Where is the line between investigative journalism and exploitation?
