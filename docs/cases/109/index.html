Case No.109

Kristian Hermansen & Matt Ploessel / Covered California

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2014
Chilling effect

The researchers found security flaws in Covered California, the state's health-insurance exchange website. Their video about the flaws was taken down from YouTube, and the FBI visited them and asked them to stop discussing the issues.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Government pressure (no charges)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Public video first versus private report first for a government health site holding sensitive data. (ii) Is an FBI request to stop talking a legitimate safety measure or intimidation? (iii) How should researchers balance public trust in a new public program against users' right to know?
