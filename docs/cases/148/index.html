Case No.148

Kurt Grutzmacher / Hewlett-Packard (H3C / Huawei routers)

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2012
Chilling effect

Grutzmacher reported SNMP flaws in H3C and Huawei networking gear to US-CERT on August 6, 2012, and told the vendors they had until his ToorCon talk. Days before the conference, HP, H3C's parent company, asked him not to present because it was not ready; he agreed to cancel. HP published its advisory on October 22, and Grutzmacher published his findings the next day.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Talk cancelled (vendor request)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should researchers honor a polite vendor request to cancel when the vendor had months of notice? (ii) Was the request about protecting users or about the vendor's own timing? (iii) Does agreeing to cancel set an expectation that makes future requests harder to refuse?
