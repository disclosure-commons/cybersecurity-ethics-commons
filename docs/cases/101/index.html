Case No.101

Henrik Høyer / Infoba (Denmark)

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2015–2017
Chilling effect

Høyer found vulnerabilities in the system his son's kindergarten used. He was accused of accessing other children's sensitive information, which he denied, and was charged rather than the flaws being fixed. He was convicted in local court and acquitted on appeal in March 2017 for lack of evidence.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Convicted (later overturned)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) A parent checking a system holding their child's data: protective duty or unauthorized access? (ii) How far can a researcher verify a flaw involving children's records? (iii) Should prosecutors pursue the reporter when the vendor's security is the real failure?
