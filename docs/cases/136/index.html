Case No.136

David Maynor & Robert Graham (Errata Security) / TippingPoint

Dual-Use Research · 2007

Errata Security reverse-engineered TippingPoint's intrusion-prevention rule set to identify the vulnerabilities the rules protected against, drawing a threat from TippingPoint. The bulk of the research was later published at Black Hat 2007.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Deriving unpatched vulnerabilities from a vendor's protection signatures: defensive research or zero-day harvesting? (ii) Does publishing the method endanger customers relying on those rules? (iii) Can IPS vendors keep signatures confidential without slowing defense?
