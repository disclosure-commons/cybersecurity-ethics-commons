Case No.094

John Jackson / Talkspace

Institutional Suppression · 2020

Jackson found that promo codes belonging to companies that had paid for Talkspace's therapy service let anyone sign up for free. Talkspace denied the claim and sent him a cease-and-desist letter.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is a business-logic flaw like promo-code abuse a security vulnerability worth disclosing, or a billing issue? (ii) Proving the flaw meant using another company's paid codes: necessary proof or misuse? (iii) Should a mental-health platform's denial and legal threat be weighed against its users' trust?
