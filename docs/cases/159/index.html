Case No.159

Apple vs. FBI / San Bernardino iPhone

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2015–2016

after the 2015 San Bernardino attack, the FBI recovered an iPhone used by one of the attackers the phone was locked and could erase its data after repeated wrong passcodes in February 2016 a court ordered Apple to help the FBI get around those protections Apple refused, arguing it would have to build a weakened version of its own software the FBI later said an outside party had unlocked the phone and dropped the case

Government / law enforcement
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Public legal dispute
FBI withdrew court order after third-party access

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Can a company be forced to weaken its own security? (ii) One phone vs. every user’s security: what responsibility does the outside party that unlocked it have?
