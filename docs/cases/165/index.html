Case No.165

Log4Shell / Apache Log4j

Dual-Use Research · 2021

Log4Shell (CVE-2021-44228), disclosed in December 2021, was a critical remote-code-execution flaw in the widely used Apache Log4j logging library. Maintainers issued urgent fixes while organizations struggled to locate affected dependencies and attackers rapidly exploited vulnerable systems. The incident highlighted the burden placed on volunteer-led open-source projects.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Emergency patching and widespread exploitation

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Who should bear responsibility for flaws in widely used volunteer-run software? (ii) Should disclosure be delayed, or sped up, once exploitation starts? (iii) What do companies owe the open source projects they depend on?
