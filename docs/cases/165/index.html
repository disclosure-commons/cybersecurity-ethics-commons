Case No.165

Log4Shell / Apache Log4j

Dual-Use Research · 2021

in late 2021 a critical remote-code-execution vulnerability (CVE-2021-44228) was disclosed in the Log4j logging library it was reported privately to Apache by a researcher at Alibaba Cloud, and the fix was being developed when exploitation began in the wild Log4j is maintained by volunteers and was embedded in a huge number of products organizations struggled just to find where they used it maintainers worked through the holidays on multiple patches as follow-up flaws appeared the vulnerability was exploited widely within days

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Emergency patching and widespread exploitation

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Who should bear responsibility for flaws in widely used volunteer-run software? (ii) Should disclosure be delayed, or sped up, once exploitation starts? (iii) What do companies owe the open source projects they depend on?
