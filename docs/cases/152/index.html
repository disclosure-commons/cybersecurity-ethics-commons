Case No.152

Raoul Chiesa / ATM Vendors

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2010

Initial reports said ATM vendors threatened Chiesa into cancelling a Hack in the Box Europe talk. Chiesa said no threats were made and the cancellation was logistical; some in the industry called it a publicity stunt.

Independent / contractor
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
None (withheld)
Talk cancelled (no threat, per researcher)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) How should media and the community verify suppression claims before amplifying them? (ii) Does publicity-driven framing harm researchers who face real threats? (iii) Should ATM research be presented publicly at all before banks patch?
