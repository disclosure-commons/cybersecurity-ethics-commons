Case No.086

Denis Tokarev (illusionofchaos) / Apple Security Bounty

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2021
Chilling effect

Tokarev submitted four iOS vulnerabilities to Apple's bug bounty between March and May 2021. After one was patched silently and he received neither credit nor payment, he published three unpatched zero-days with proof-of-concept code. Apple later sent DMCA takedowns over a documentation site he had mirrored, and credited one bug (CVE-2021-30871) in October 2021.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Vendor non-responsive → public disclosure
Civil threat (DMCA takedowns)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Does a vendor breaking its bounty promises justify releasing unpatched zero-days that expose millions of users? (ii) Were the later DMCA takedowns legitimate IP enforcement or retaliation for the disclosure? (iii) Should bounty programs be held to enforceable timelines for credit and payment?
