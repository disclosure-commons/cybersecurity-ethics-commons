Case No.007

Princeton University

Informed Consent & Human Subjects · ~2017

Researchers studying web censorship placed JavaScript snippets on third-party websites; visiting users' browsers silently participated in the experiment without consent. Users in authoritarian countries faced potential state surveillance.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) No informed consent from users whose computers were co-opted; (ii) Placing users at physical/legal risk in authoritarian regimes; (iii) Public interest vs. individual rights; (iv) IRB adequacy for research externalizing risk onto bystanders

Source
