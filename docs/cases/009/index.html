Case No.009

University College London (UCL)

Informed Consent & Human Subjects · ~2014

Researchers passively captured packets on open public Wi-Fi networks to analyze what personally identifiable information was transmitted in cleartext, finding widespread exposure of emails, login credentials, and location data.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Legality of passive interception of open Wi-Fi traffic (Wiretap Act / ECPA); (ii) Whether 'no login required' implies user consent to interception; (iii) IRB adequacy for research intercepting third-party communications; (iv) Publication alerting both attackers and defenders

Source
