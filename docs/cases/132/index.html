Case No.132

Secunia / Autonomy (KeyView)

Institutional Suppression · 2007

Secunia published vulnerabilities in Autonomy's KeyView after Autonomy threatened legal action. Autonomy reportedly threatened CORE Security as well, which was not made public.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should researchers publicize threats they receive, or handle them quietly? (ii) Is it fair to the field when one firm resists a threat and another stays silent? (iii) Can legal threats against a widely embedded library ever serve its downstream users?
