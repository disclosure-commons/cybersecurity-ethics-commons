Case No.160

Kaseya VSA ransomware supply-chain attack

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2021

In July 2021, the REvil group exploited vulnerabilities in Kaseya's VSA management software, spreading ransomware through managed service providers to downstream organizations. Dutch researchers had reported related flaws to Kaseya before the attack. The incident highlighted the difficulty of coordinating vulnerability disclosure and patch deployment across complex software supply chains.

Cybercriminal group
Malicious / reckless (knowingly harmful)
None (withheld)
Ransomware incident; recovery and law-enforcement actions

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) How quickly should vendors act when researchers report vulnerabilities in software used by many downstream organizations?; (ii) What responsibilities do managed service providers have to their customers during supply-chain incidents?; (iii) Should a patch timeline be accelerated when exploitation appears imminent?; (iv) How should vendors and researchers coordinate warnings without aiding attackers?
