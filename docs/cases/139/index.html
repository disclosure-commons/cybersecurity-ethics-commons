Case No.139

Billy Hoffman & Virgil Griffith / Blackboard Transaction System

Institutional Suppression · 2003
Chilling effect

Blackboard sent a cease-and-desist to the Interz0ne conference and filed a complaint against two students who planned to present flaws in its campus card transaction system. The parties reached a confidential agreement.

Student
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil suit / Injunction

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should vendors be able to stop students presenting flaws in campus systems they use daily? (ii) Do confidential settlements protect students or bury research? (iii) Does a vendor's repeated conflict with student researchers (see also Demirkapi) suggest a pattern worth weighing?
