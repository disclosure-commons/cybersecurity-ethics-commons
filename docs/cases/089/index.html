Case No.089

Lilith Wittmann / CDU connect app (Germany)

Institutional Suppression · 2021
Chilling effect

During Germany's 2021 federal election campaign, Wittmann found and published data-security problems in the campaign app used by the CDU, CSU and Austria's ÖVP. The CDU filed a criminal complaint against her, then withdrew it and apologized after public outcry.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Criminal complaint (withdrawn, apology)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Publishing flaws in a party's app during an election: public interest in voter-data security versus influence on the campaign. (ii) Should political parties be held to the same disclosure expectations as vendors? (iii) Does Germany's anti-hacking law leave good-faith researchers too exposed to complaints like this?
