Case No.053

David Ross ('Connor Goodwolf') / City of Columbus Ransomware

Institutional Suppression · 2024.0
Chilling effect

After Columbus downplayed a Rhysida ransomware breach, independent researcher Ross shared proof with local media that police personnel files, undercover officer identities, and 400,000+ residents' data were exposed. Columbus sued him and obtained a TRO; the city dropped the suit ~two months later under pressure from the EFF.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Civil suit (dropped)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Suing a whistleblower to manage a government's own breach-disclosure narrative; (ii) First Amendment limits on restraining someone from discussing already-public leaked data; (iii) Power asymmetry between a city government and an individual researcher
