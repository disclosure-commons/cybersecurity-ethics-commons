Case No.053

In Re Okta, Inc. Securities Litigation

Financial Conflict of Interest · 2022

After screenshots posted by Lapsus$ in March 2022 revealed an earlier compromise involving an Okta customer-support contractor, Okta faced criticism over the timing and clarity of its public disclosures. Investors filed securities litigation alleging misleading statements about security and the incident. A separate 2023 breach of Okta's support system should not be conflated with this case.

Corporate / private sector
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
None (withheld)
Securities class action; settlement

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) When a company minimizes a breach publicly while knowing the full scope internally, is that fraud or legitimate incident response?; (ii) What is the appropriate disclosure timeline when breach scope is still being investigated?; (iii) Does downplaying a breach to avoid stock impact constitute securities fraud even if the company later corrects the record?; (iv) How should security executives balance legal counsel's advice against ethical obligations to customers?
