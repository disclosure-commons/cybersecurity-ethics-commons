Case No.104

Secunia / VideoLAN (VLC)

Institutional Suppression · 2013

Secunia reported a use-after-free bug in VLC Media Player. VideoLAN threatened legal action and disputed whether it was patched; Secunia changed its advisory to 'patched' and then back to 'unpatched' after further analysis, and both sides published accounts of the dispute.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible / coordinated
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) When vendor and researcher disagree on whether a bug is fixed, whose claim should users trust? (ii) Can an open-source project threaten legal action without undermining its own community norms? (iii) Should an advisory ever change status under legal pressure?
