Johns Hopkins University (High-Interaction Honeypots)

Dual-Use Research · 2024.0

JHU researchers deployed high-interaction honeypots to study real attacker behavior and TTPs in depth.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Ethical interaction with cybercriminals without encouraging illegal behavior; (ii) Entrapment vs. observation; (iii) Non-consent experimentation on criminals
