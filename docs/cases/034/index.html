Case No.034

IOActive / Smart City Infrastructure Research

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2015–2018

IOActive researchers found critical flaws in smart city infrastructure — traffic sensors, emergency-alert systems, water-control systems — through some unauthorized testing ('fire and forget' scan-and-test). Vendors were slow to patch; IOActive published technical details at DEF CON.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Scanning municipal critical infrastructure without written permission; (ii) Full technical disclosure of infrastructure vulnerabilities when patches remain unavailable; (iii) Risk that published details of water/traffic attacks could cause physical harm; (iv) Whether researchers must wait longer when targets are critical infrastructure
