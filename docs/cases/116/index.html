Case No.116

Ahmed Al-Khabaz / Dawson College & Skytech (Omnivox)

Institutional Suppression · 2013
Chilling effect

Al-Khabaz, a student, found a flaw in the Omnivox portal that exposed about 250,000 students' records and reported it to his college. Skytech threatened to press charges and send him to jail unless he signed an NDA, and he was later expelled.

Student
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Expelled (no charges)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is re-testing a reported system to check the fix diligence or new unauthorized access? (ii) Is demanding an NDA under threat of prosecution ever acceptable? (iii) Should a school discipline a student for reporting a flaw in software it bought?
