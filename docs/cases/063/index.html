Case No.063

IT Consultant Fined for Exposing Shoddy Security (Germany / Modern Solution parallel)

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2024
Chilling effect

A German IT consultant discovered a security vulnerability at a client's system and reported it. Instead of addressing the flaw, the company reported the consultant to police. The consultant was fined for unauthorized access under German law, even though their intent was to expose and report the vulnerability. The case closely parallels the Modern Solution GmbH case (C056) and reflects a broader pattern in German jurisprudence of criminalizing good-faith vulnerability verification.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Convicted

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should accessing a system to verify the scope of a vulnerability you discovered constitute criminal 'unauthorized access'?; (ii) Is reporting a security flaw to the company that hired you sufficient authorization to investigate it?; (iii) How does German computer crime law compare to the CFAA in its treatment of good-faith security researchers?; (iv) What legal safe harbors should exist for contractors who discover vulnerabilities in systems they are hired to work on?
