Case No.019

Marcus Hutchins / WannaCry / Kronos Malware

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2017
Chilling effect

Hutchins stopped the WannaCry ransomware by registering its kill-switch domain. Months later the FBI arrested him at DEF CON, alleging he helped develop the Kronos banking trojan in 2014–2015. He pleaded guilty to two CFAA counts and was sentenced to time served.

Independent / contractor
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
None (withheld)
Convicted (time served)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should past black-hat activity negate subsequent white-hat contributions?; (ii) Arresting a researcher at a security conference as a deterrent tactic; (iii) Fairness of prosecuting juvenile/early-career offenses years later; (iv) Community trust in law enforcement collaboration with researchers
