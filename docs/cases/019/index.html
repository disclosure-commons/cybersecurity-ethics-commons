Case No.019

Ola Bini / Ecuador / Privacy Tool Developer

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2019
Chilling effect

Swedish programmer and privacy advocate Ola Bini was arrested in Ecuador in 2019. His 2023 acquittal was overturned on appeal. According to EFF’s October 2026 report, the criminal case subsequently expired under the statute of limitations and precautionary measures were lifted. Ecuadorian authorities then revoked his visa and ordered his immediate deportation, drawing renewed criticism from digital-rights groups.

Journalist / activist / independent
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
Criminal case expired under statute of limitations; deportation ordered (October 2026).

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Criminalization of possession of security tools and technical literature as evidence of malicious intent; (ii) Politically motivated prosecution of security researchers; (iii) Due process rights of open-source developers; (iv) Chilling effect on privacy-tool development
