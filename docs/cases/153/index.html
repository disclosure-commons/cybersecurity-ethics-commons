Case No.153

Barnaby Jack (Juniper Networks) / ATM Vendor

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2009–2010

Juniper Networks pulled Barnaby Jack's Black Hat 2009 talk, 'Jackpotting Automated Teller Machines,' after the affected ATM vendor said its customers were not yet protected; Jack had reportedly worked with the vendor for months. After moving to IOActive, he demonstrated ATM jackpotting attacks at Black Hat 2010; Triton said it had patched in November 2009.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Talk postponed one year

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is a one-year delay a reasonable trade for giving operators time to patch ATMs? (ii) Should an employer, rather than the researcher, decide when a talk is ready? (iii) Does a live jackpotting demo inform defenders or instruct criminals?
