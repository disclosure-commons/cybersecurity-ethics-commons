Case No.166

Vault 7 / CIA cyber-tool disclosures

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2017
Chilling effect

in 2017 WikiLeaks began publishing “Vault 7,” documents describing CIA hacking tools and techniques the material covered exploits for phones, computers, and smart TVs WikiLeaks said it withheld some code and redacted parts, but the documents still described capabilities in detail the CIA said the leak harmed its operations and security a CIA software engineer was later convicted for leaking the material to WikiLeaks supporters said the public should know about government hacking, critics said the disclosure helped adversaries

Government / intelligence
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Media-first / leak
Criminal prosecution of source; public disclosures

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is publishing details of government hacking tools in the public interest or a security risk? (ii) How much should a publisher redact? (iii) Is the leaker a whistleblower or a criminal?
