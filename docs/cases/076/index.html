Case No.076

Timnit Gebru / Google / "Stochastic Parrots"

Institutional Suppression · 2020.0
Chilling effect

Google researcher co-authored a paper discussing risks of large language models. Google demanded retraction, and after refusing, Gebru was forced out of the company.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Corporate influence over AI ethics research; (ii) Academic freedom vs. employer interests; (iii) Suppression of critical AI research; (iv) Research independence
