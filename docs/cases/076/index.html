Case No.076

Erik Johnson / Proctorio

Institutional Suppression · 2020–2022
Chilling effect

Student and critic Erik Johnson published material criticizing Proctorio’s remote exam-monitoring software. Proctorio used copyright claims against excerpts used in criticism; the ensuing legal dispute raised concerns about fair use, surveillance, and attempts to silence scrutiny.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Civil suit / Injunction

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Abuse of copyright law; (ii) Research criticism; (iii) Chilling effects
