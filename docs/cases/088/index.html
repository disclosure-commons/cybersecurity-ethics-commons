Case No.088

Ben Sadeghipour (NahamSec) / Ford via Rapid7

Institutional Suppression · 2023

Rapid7, acting on Ford's behalf in a brand-protection effort, emailed NahamSec claiming one of his YouTube videos was illegal and that he was aiding criminals, and asked him to remove it. He replied that he had done nothing wrong and declined to remove it immediately.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Where does educational hacking content end and enabling fraud begin? (ii) Should brand-protection services treat security education as a threat by default? (iii) Should a security company take takedown work aimed at other researchers?
