Case No.052

In Re Yahoo! Inc. Customer Data Security Breach Litigation

Financial Conflict of Interest · 2013–2020

Lawsuits followed major Yahoo account breaches from 2013 onward and allegations of insufficient cybersecurity and delayed notification. Consumers challenged whether the company adequately protected and informed its users.

Corporate / private sector
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
None (withheld)
Civil suit / Injunction

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) At what point does delayed breach disclosure cross from incident response into material omission for securities fraud?; (ii) What is a CSO/CISO's personal duty when corporate leadership decides to delay disclosure?; (iii) Acquisition context: does a pending merger change the calculus of when a breach must be disclosed?; (iv) Are current securities laws adequate to deter corporate concealment of large-scale breaches?
