Case No.052

Charlie Miller & Chris Valasek / Jeep Cherokee

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2015–2019

Researchers remotely disabled a Jeep's transmission and brakes over its cellular-connected infotainment system while a journalist drove on the highway. This forced a recall of ~1.4 million vehicles; owners filed a class-action suit against FCA alleging the company knew of the architectural flaw since 2011.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Provocation (public stunt to force recall)
No charges filed; civil suit allowed to proceed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is a dramatic public stunt the only way to force a slow-moving manufacturer to patch a safety-critical flaw?; (ii) Consumer standing to sue over a vulnerability patched before real-world exploitation; (iii) Coupling safety-critical vehicle systems to internet-facing infotainment
