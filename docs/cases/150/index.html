Case No.150

Riley Hassell & Shane Macaulay / Google Android

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2011

The Privateer Labs researchers did not appear for their scheduled Black Hat 2011 talk, 'Hacking Androids for Profit.' Hassell later said they withdrew to prevent an unacceptable window of risk to consumers and to make sure prior research was credited, and that they had alerted Google. Google said the identified bugs were not present in Android.

Corporate / private sector
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Responsible/coordinated
Talk withdrawn

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is withdrawing a talk over misuse fears responsible, or does it leave users uninformed? (ii) How should researchers handle discovering their work overlaps prior research? (iii) What should a vendor's public denial mean for the research's credibility?
