Case No.016

Harvard University (Mobile App Reverse Engineering)

Dual-Use Research · ~2012

Harvard researchers reverse-engineered popular mobile applications to expose privacy risks and data leaks, violating app store terms of service and potentially IP rights in the process.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is reverse engineering software to expose privacy violations legally and ethically permissible?; (ii) Violation of ToS and IP rights in public-interest research; (iii) Responsible disclosure when app developers deny wrongdoing; (iv) IRB adequacy for research involving third-party software artifacts
