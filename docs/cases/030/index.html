Case No.030

Crisis Text Line / Data Sharing with Loris.ai

Informed Consent & Human Subjects · 2022.0
Chilling effect

Crisis Text Line, a mental-health crisis nonprofit, shared detailed conversation data with Loris.ai, a for-profit spinoff, to train AI models sold to corporations. The arrangement was buried in the privacy policy; public outrage and investigations followed.

Corporate / private sector
Financially motivated (profit, stock, bounty)
None (withheld)
Regulatory investigation

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Informed consent: crisis users cannot meaningfully consent to commercial AI training of their data; (ii) Re-identification risk from 'anonymized' highly sensitive mental health data; (iii) Conflict of interest when nonprofit mission is compromised by commercial data partnerships; (iv) Whether 'disclosed in privacy policy' constitutes adequate consent
