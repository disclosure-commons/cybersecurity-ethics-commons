Case No.073

Andrew Lemon / Traffic Signal Controller Disclosure

Institutional Suppression · 2024
Chilling effect

Security researcher Andrew Lemon reported that he found a critical vulnerability in a traffic-signal controller he had purchased secondhand. According to his public account, the vendor's lawyers responded with threats of prosecution and disputed his authorization to test the equipment, rather than engaging directly with the reported issue.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible / coordinated
Legal threat alleged by researcher

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Legal intimidation; (ii) Responsible disclosure; (iii) Chilling effect
