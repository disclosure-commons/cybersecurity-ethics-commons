Case No.091

Rob Dyke / Apperta Foundation (NHS-backed)

Institutional Suppression · 2021
Chilling effect

Dyke found sensitive information in a public GitHub repository of the Apperta Foundation, an NHS-backed nonprofit, and reported it. Apperta reported him to Northumbria Police and revoked licenses on materials he used.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Police report (no charges reported)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) How much should a researcher look at exposed data to confirm a leak before reporting it? (ii) Reporting the finder to police: reasonable caution or blame-shifting by the organization that leaked? (iii) Do publicly funded health projects owe more transparency about their own mistakes?
