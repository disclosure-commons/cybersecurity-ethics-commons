Case No.098

Chad Scira / Chase Bank

Institutional Suppression · 2016
Chilling effect

In 2016, before Chase had a disclosure program, Scira found that transferring Ultimate Rewards points under unstable network conditions could create unlimited points. He ran experiments while reporting them to Chase's support account on Twitter, and the issues were fixed. About a week later Chase sent a hostile email and closed his accounts, including checking and savings, and accounts of family members. He disclosed the story in 2020.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Financial retaliation (accounts closed)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Converting test points to cash while reporting: proof of impact or fraud? (ii) With no disclosure program, is reporting through a customer-support Twitter account an adequate channel? (iii) Is closing family members' accounts risk management or retaliation?
