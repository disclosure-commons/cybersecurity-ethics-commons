Case No.047

Bill Demirkapi / Blackboard & Follett (Age 16)

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2017–2019

A 16-year-old found SQL injection and access-control flaws exposing ~5 million student records. After Blackboard ignored him for months and Follett never responded, he pushed a mass notification to students, parents, and staff. Blackboard required an NDA before further conversation; the school suspended him for two days.

Student
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Vendor non-responsive → mass user notification
Suspended from school (no criminal charges)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is escalating to a mass alert ethical when normal disclosure channels are ignored by vendors?; (ii) Vendors using NDAs to control a minor researcher's ability to discuss findings; (iii) Industry-wide underinvestment in security for children's data
