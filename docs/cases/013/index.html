Case No.013

DarkMatter / Project Raven / Ex-NSA Operatives

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2019–2022
Chilling effect

Three former U.S. intelligence and military personnel participated in cyber operations supporting the United Arab Emirates through Project Raven. The operation used sophisticated surveillance and intrusion capabilities against targets including dissidents, journalists, and U.S. persons. Their activities led to U.S. legal and administrative enforcement actions.

Government / law enforcement
Malicious / reckless (knowingly harmful)
None (withheld)
Deferred prosecution agreement

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Cyber-mercenaries using US intelligence training to oppress foreign dissidents; (ii) Export control violations (ITAR) for offensive cyber tools; (iii) Accountability gap: deferred prosecution lets former intelligence officers pay their way out; (iv) Revolving door between intelligence agencies and private offensive-cyber firms
