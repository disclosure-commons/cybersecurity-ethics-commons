Case No.117

Ralph Langner / Unnamed ICS Vendor

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2012
Chilling effect

Langner's talk on nuclear power plant SCADA vulnerabilities was cancelled at the last minute at the 12th ICS Cyber Security Conference after a vendor objected and threatened to sue, even though plant officials had approved the presentation.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Research publication
Civil threat (talk cancelled)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) If the plant operator approves disclosure, should the equipment vendor still have a veto? (ii) How much detail about nuclear-plant control systems is safe for a public audience? (iii) Does silencing ICS research make plants safer or just less informed?
