Case No.157

Joshua Schulte / Vault 7 / CIA cyber-tool disclosures

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2017
Chilling effect

In 2017, WikiLeaks began publishing documents about CIA cyber tools under the name Vault 7, revealing techniques used against computers, phones, and connected devices. The disclosures stimulated public debate but also raised concerns about exposing offensive capabilities. Former CIA engineer Joshua Schulte was later convicted of crimes connected to the theft and disclosure of the files.

Government / intelligence
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Media-first / leak
Criminal prosecution of source; public disclosures

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is publishing details of government hacking tools in the public interest or a security risk? (ii) How much should a publisher redact? (iii) Is the leaker a whistleblower or a criminal?
