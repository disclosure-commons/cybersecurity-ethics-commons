Case No.157

Stuxnet

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2010

Stuxnet, discovered in 2010, was malware designed to disrupt industrial control equipment associated with uranium enrichment at Iran's Natanz facility. It is widely attributed to the United States and Israel, although neither has publicly acknowledged responsibility. Its spread beyond the intended environment illustrated how offensive cyber operations can create risks outside their targets.

Government / intelligence
Malicious / reckless (knowingly harmful)
None (withheld)
No public criminal charges

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is a cyberattack on infrastructure different from a conventional strike? (ii) Is it acceptable to hoard and use zero-days that leave everyone else vulnerable? (iii) Who is accountable when the code escapes its target?
