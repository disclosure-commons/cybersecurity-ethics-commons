Case No.008

Carnegie Mellon University (CMU / SEI)

Informed Consent & Human Subjects · 2014.0
Chilling effect

CMU researchers discovered a technique to de-anonymize Tor users by running malicious relay nodes. They planned to present at Black Hat but canceled. The FBI allegedly paid ~$1M to use the technique against Silk Road 2.0 users, sweeping thousands of non-criminal Tor users.

Academic researcher
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
None (withheld)
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Research on human subjects without consent; (ii) Withholding a vulnerability from the affected party (Tor Project); (iii) Financial incentives vs. ethical responsibility; (iv) Using academic research as outsourced law enforcement
