Case No.070

Suchir Balaji / OpenAI

Institutional Suppression · 2024
Chilling effect

Former OpenAI engineer Suchir Balaji publicly challenged the legality and ethics of training commercial generative AI systems on copyrighted works, arguing that resulting tools may compete with creators whose works they use.
