Case No.147

Operation Trojan Shield / ANOM

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2018

Under Operation Trojan Shield, the FBI and international partners secretly operated the ANOM encrypted-phone platform and distributed it through criminal networks. Messages were copied to investigators, leading to coordinated arrests announced in 2021. The operation raised questions about covert monitoring and cross-border legal safeguards.

Government / law enforcement
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
Law-enforcement operation; mass arrests

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is it acceptable for police to run a deceptive “secure” product? (ii) Should agencies be able to route around domestic legal limits through foreign partners? (iii) Does this make people less willing to trust real privacy tools?
