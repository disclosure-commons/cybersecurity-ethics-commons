Case No.147

Mansour Moufid / Carleton University

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2008
Chilling effect

A student used keyloggers on university computers to collect other students' credentials in order to expose security weaknesses, and was charged with computer crimes. disclose.io lists this among incidents that crossed the line.

Student
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Report to institution
Charged

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Can collecting real users' credentials ever be justified to prove a system is insecure? (ii) Does a good motive excuse harm to classmates who never consented? (iii) What should a student do when an institution ignores warnings?
