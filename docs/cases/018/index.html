Case No.018

University of Michigan / FTP: The Forgotten Cloud

Dual-Use Research · 2015

University of Michigan researchers surveyed internet-facing FTP servers and found approximately 13.8 million servers, including 1.1 million allowing anonymous access. Their study documented exposures of sensitive files and the misuse of publicly writable FTP directories, raising questions about the ethics of internet-wide measurement.

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible / coordinated
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Ethical permissibility of internet-wide scanning without operator consent; (ii) Legal exposure under CFAA for automated access to systems not owned by researchers; (iii) Responsible disclosure when 1M+ vulnerable systems cannot all be notified; (iv) Dual-use: FBI used findings, but so did ransomware actors
