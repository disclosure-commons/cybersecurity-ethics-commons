Case No.018

NSO Group / Pegasus Spyware

Surveillance & Offensive Tools · 2016–2021
Chilling effect

NSO Group sold Pegasus zero-click exploit suite to governments worldwide. A 2021 investigation found 50,000+ surveillance targets including journalists, human rights defenders, and opposition leaders across 45+ countries.

Corporate / private sector
Malicious / reckless (knowingly harmful)
None (withheld)
Sanctions / export restrictions (no criminal charges)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Ethics of selling powerful offensive cyber tools to authoritarian governments; (ii) Dual-use technology: legitimate law enforcement vs. repression of civil society; (iii) Liability of tool creators for end-user misuse; (iv) Whether private companies should be permitted to develop nation-state-grade offensive spyware

Source

https://securitylab.amnesty.org/case-study-the-pegasus-project/
