Case No.108

Jean-Marie Bourbon / FireEye

Institutional Suppression · 2014
Chilling effect

After Bourbon sent details of vulnerabilities in FireEye's Malware Analysis System to Exploit-DB, he was suspended from his day job, reportedly under pressure from FireEye, which denied involvement. FireEye later patched the issues and credited him.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Employer suspension (no charges)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is sending findings straight to a public exploit database fair to the vendor's customers? (ii) Should a vendor ever contact a researcher's employer over independent work? (iii) Should employers shield staff from vendor pressure over research done on their own time?
