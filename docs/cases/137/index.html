Case No.137

Vipsta & MurderSkillz / SimpleBlog (8pixel.net)

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2006

Two researchers reported a SQL injection flaw in the SimpleBlog Drupal theme and were threatened with legal action the same day. Two days later they released an exploit on Exploit-DB.

Independent / contractor
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Media-first
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is releasing an exploit two days after a legal threat retaliation or protection of users? (ii) Should a threat end a researcher's duty to coordinate? (iii) How should small open-source theme authors handle reports they don't understand?
