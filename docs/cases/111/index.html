Case No.111

Daniel Wood / ZippyYum (Subway ordering app)

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2013

Wood published an insecure data storage issue in ZippyYum's iOS Subway ordering app on the Full Disclosure mailing list. Anticipating legal action, he noted he had signed no NDA and retained an attorney.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Civil threat

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Full disclosure on a public list versus private notice for a consumer payment app. (ii) Should researchers retain counsel before publishing? (iii) Do customers' interests favor speed or coordination when card data may be stored insecurely?
