Case No.067

Timnit Gebru / Google / "Stochastic Parrots"

Institutional Suppression · 2020
Chilling effect

AI ethics researcher Timnit Gebru disputed Google management over a research paper criticizing the risks and limitations of large language models. Her December 2020 departure sparked a public debate about corporate influence on critical research.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Corporate influence over AI ethics research; (ii) Academic freedom vs. employer interests; (iii) Suppression of critical AI research; (iv) Research independence
