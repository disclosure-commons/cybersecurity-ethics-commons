Case No.036

Paige Thompson / Capital One / AWS

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2019

Former Amazon Web Services employee Paige Thompson exploited a misconfiguration in Capital One's cloud environment in 2019 to obtain credentials and copy data affecting roughly 100 million people in the United States and six million in Canada. Prosecutors also alleged intrusions into other organizations and unauthorized cryptocurrency mining. She was convicted of wire fraud and computer-access offenses and was sentenced to time served, supervision, and restitution.

Corporate / private sector
Malicious / reckless (knowingly harmful)
None (withheld)
Convicted; time served, supervised release, and $40.745 million restitution

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Where is the line between legitimate cloud-misconfiguration research and felony data theft?; (ii) Should mental health and personal circumstances factor into sentencing for large-scale breaches?; (iii) Appellate oversight of 'too lenient' sentences for first-time offenders
