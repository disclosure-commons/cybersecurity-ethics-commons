Case No.036

Radboud University / Bart Jacobs (NXP / Mifare Classic)

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2008

- the university investigated transit chips manufactured by NXP - chips are used for public transit, building access badges by using a encryption algo that they kept a secret - researchers reverse engineered it and was able to get secret keys and clone cards - in march 2008 they told the dutch gov and NXP the information so they could fix it, but thye wanted to publish a paper about it in June - July 2008 NXP took the reserachers to court to stop the publication - it was denied, researchers said they should let the public know about the technologies they use, court said freedom of expression is needed

Academic researcher
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Civil suit (court ruled for researchers)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should commercial harm from a disclosed flaw ever justify suppressing peer-reviewed research?; (ii) Academic freedom vs. critical-infrastructure (transit) security concerns; (iii) Adequacy of advance confidential notification before publication
