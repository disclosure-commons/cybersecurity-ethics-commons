Case No.118

Eric Romang / E-Soft

Institutional Suppression · 2012

E-Soft sent YouTube a copyright claim to remove Romang's video demonstrating a Metasploit exploit module for its Digital Music Pad software. The video was reposted and remained available.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Civil threat (copyright takedown)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is a copyright claim against an exploit demo a legitimate use of IP law? (ii) Are video demonstrations of working exploits a responsible way to publicize flaws? (iii) Should platforms review takedowns aimed at security research?
