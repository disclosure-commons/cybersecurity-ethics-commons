Case No.102

Gianni Gnesa / Surveillance Camera Vendor

Institutional Suppression · 2015
Chilling effect

Gnesa withdrew his Hack in the Box GSEC talk on surveillance camera vulnerabilities after a legal threat from one of the three vendors involved, despite having privately disclosed the flaws to the vendor in advance.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Civil threat (talk withdrawn)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) If a vendor was warned privately, does it still have standing to block a talk? (ii) Should researchers withdraw talks under threat, or present and risk a lawsuit? (iii) Who protects the camera owners while the research stays unpublished?
