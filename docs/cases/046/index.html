Case No.046

Dragon Sector / Newag SA (Poland)

Institutional Suppression · 2025
Chilling effect

After Newag-built passenger trains repeatedly stopped working following maintenance by independent repair shops, Polish research group Dragon Sector examined the train software and identified code associated with location- and servicing-related lockouts. Newag denied wrongdoing and pursued legal claims against the researchers. The dispute highlights the tensions among software inspection, right to repair, and railway safety.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Civil suit (ongoing)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Manufacturers using anti-repair 'DRM' embedded in safety-critical infrastructure; (ii) Suing researchers for exposing the lockout rather than addressing it (right-to-repair); (iii) Criminal investigations opened against disclosers rather than the manufacturer
