Case No.046

Michael Lynn / Cisco / Black Hat 2005

Institutional Suppression · 2005.0
Chilling effect

Lynn resigned from ISS on the morning of his Black Hat talk to warn that Cisco routers could be exploited for a self-propagating 'router worm.' Cisco and ISS obtained a federal injunction barring further dissemination; Lynn agreed to a permanent injunction surrendering all related materials.

Corporate / private sector
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Civil injunction (permanent)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Using copyright/trade-secret claims to suppress a security warning after the fact; (ii) Researcher's duty to disclose imminent internet-scale risk vs. employer's IP claims; (iii) Chilling effect of corporate legal muscle on conference speakers
