Case No.054

In Re Equifax Inc. Securities Litigation

Financial Conflict of Interest · 2017–2020

Securities investors sued Equifax and executives following its massive 2017 data breach, alleging misleading statements and omissions concerning cybersecurity practices and exposure to risk.

Corporate / private sector
Malicious / reckless (knowingly harmful)
None (withheld)
Convicted

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Does selling stock after learning of a breach but before public disclosure constitute insider trading, regardless of intent?; (ii) What personal criminal liability do security executives bear for their company's breach concealment?; (iii) Should a CISO who discovers a breach be required to immediately notify the board even if doing so triggers premature disclosure?; (iv) Are mandatory breach disclosure timelines the right policy lever, or do they just create liability without improving security?
