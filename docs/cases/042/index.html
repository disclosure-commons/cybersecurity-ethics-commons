Case No.042

Joseph Sullivan / Uber Data Breach Cover-Up

Financial Conflict of Interest · 2016–2023
Chilling effect

Ten days after testifying to the FTC about Uber's security, Sullivan learned of a breach of 57M records. He arranged a $100K payment to hackers framed as a bug bounty and concealed the breach from the FTC. Convicted of obstruction and misprision of a felony; sentenced to three years' probation and $50K fine.

Corporate / private sector
Malicious / reckless (knowingly harmful)
None (withheld)
Convicted (probation, $50K fine, 200 hours community service)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Personal criminal liability of security executives for breach cover-ups; (ii) Where is the line between 'incident response' and witness tampering/concealment?; (iii) Should corporate leadership above the CSO share equal culpability?
