Case No.167

Edward Snowden / NSA surveillance disclosures

Institutional Suppression · 2013
Chilling effect

in 2013 Snowden, a contractor, gave journalists classified documents about US surveillance programs the documents described programs that collected phone metadata and internet communications at large scale he went to the press rather than using internal channels, saying those would not work the disclosures led to public debate and some legal and policy changes he was charged under the Espionage Act and has lived abroad, in Russia, since critics say the leak harmed intelligence work and that he went beyond exposing wrongdoing

Government / intelligence
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Media-first / leak
Charges filed; Snowden remains in exile

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is breaking secrecy obligations justified when the programs may be unlawful? (ii) Should he have used internal channels first? (iii) How should whistleblowers be protected, given there is no real public-interest defense under the Espionage Act?
