Case No.105

Joaquín Sorianello / MSA Vot.ar (Argentina)

Critical Infrastructure & Public Trust · 2015
Chilling effect

Sorianello saw a publicly posted SSL certificate flaw in the Vot.ar electronic voting system and reported it to vendor MSA as a warning; he had not found or published it. After a different group published another Vot.ar vulnerability, police raided his home on a judge's order.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Responsible/coordinated
Home raided (no charges reported)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Does reporting someone else's public finding expose the reporter to liability? (ii) During an election, should authorities treat reporters of flaws as suspects or allies? (iii) How should researchers protect themselves when warning a vendor about issues they didn't discover?
