Case No.145

Charles Edge (318 Inc.) / Apple FileVault

Institutional Suppression · 2008

Edge reportedly rescinded a Black Hat talk on FileVault encryption weaknesses after Apple invoked an existing NDA. Black Hat's CFP team found no record of the talk being submitted, and Attrition suggested the incident was used for publicity. disclose.io lists this as unconfirmed.

Corporate / private sector
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
None (withheld)
Talk withdrawn (NDA; disputed)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should an NDA with a vendor override disclosure of flaws affecting all users? (ii) How should the community treat suppression claims that may be publicity? (iii) Should researchers under NDA research that vendor's products at all?
