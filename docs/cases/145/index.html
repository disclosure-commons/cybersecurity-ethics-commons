Case No.145

Aran Khanna / Harvard University / Facebook Messenger

Informed Consent & Human Subjects · 2015
Chilling effect

Harvard student Aran Khanna developed Marauder's Map, a browser extension that plotted location coordinates attached to Facebook Messenger messages and showed how they could reveal people's movements. After the extension attracted attention, Facebook asked him to remove it and withdrew an internship offer. Facebook subsequently changed Messenger's location-sharing behavior.

Student
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Public disclosure after vendor response
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is it ethical to expose people's location just to demonstrate a privacy issue? (ii) Was publicly releasing the extension necessary? (iii) Is Facebook justified in withdrawing his internship? (iv) Is collecting existing data ethically different from creating new surveillance capabilities?
