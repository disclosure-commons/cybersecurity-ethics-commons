Case No.003

Andrew 'Weev' Auernheimer / AT&T (Arkansas)

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2010–2014
Chilling effect

Auernheimer scripted enumeration of AT&T's unsecured iPad endpoint, harvesting 114,000 email addresses and ICC-IDs. He shared the data with Gawker. Convicted under CFAA (41 months); vacated on venue grounds.

Independent / contractor
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Media-first
Convicted (later overturned/expunged)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is scraping publicly accessible but unintended data unauthorized access under CFAA?; (ii) Responsible disclosure vs. media disclosure to maximize embarrassment; (iii) Chilling effect on legitimate security research; (iv) Corporate negligence vs. researcher culpability
