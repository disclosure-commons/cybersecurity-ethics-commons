Case No.039

Daniel Cuthbert / Disaster Emergency Committee (UK)

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2004.0
Chilling effect

After donating to a tsunami relief site and receiving no confirmation page, security consultant Daniel Cuthbert manually tested the donation page for phishing signs. He was convicted under the UK Computer Misuse Act and fined — the first such conviction under that section.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
None (withheld)
Convicted (fined £400 + £600 costs)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Does intent matter under strict-liability computer-misuse statutes?; (ii) Should consumer self-protection checks ever be criminalized?; (iii) Career destruction disproportionate to harm caused
