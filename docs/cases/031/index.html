Case No.031

Bret McDanel / Tornado Development Inc.

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2000
Chilling effect

- computer security for tornado devleopment (web-based email, voicemail, fx) - found vulneratbilityin webmail system that allowed unauthroized people to access customers' private messages - the weakness was session information that could be exposed through web addresses - warned supervisions about the problem, was not fixed even after he left and was asked again to fix it - messaged 5,600 emails to tornado customers about this. -company fixed the bug but contacted the law enforcement - said he interfered with torndao servers and damaged the companies reputation - convicted under the Computer fraud and abuse act and served 16 months in prison - it was overturned due to interpretiation of law being incorrect (still served his sentence)

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Convicted (later overturned)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should warning customers about a security flaw ever count as a 'malicious code' offense?; (ii) What recourse exists for a wrongful felony conviction already served?; (iii) Chilling effect of CFAA's vague 'damage' language on good-faith disclosure
