Case No.031

Bret McDanel / Tornado Development Inc.

Legal Boundaries & CFAA Overreach · 2000
Chilling effect

Former Tornado Development employee Bret McDanel warned of a webmail flaw that could expose customers' private messages. After the company failed to address it, he emailed thousands of customers directly; prosecutors treated the mass message as a computer offense. McDanel served a prison sentence before his conviction was overturned.

Independent / contractor
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
Conviction overturned after sentence served

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Should warning customers about a security flaw ever count as a 'malicious code' offense?; (ii) What recourse exists for a wrongful felony conviction already served?; (iii) Chilling effect of CFAA's vague 'damage' language on good-faith disclosure
