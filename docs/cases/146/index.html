Case No.146

Argeniss / Oracle (Week of Oracle Bugs)

Vulnerability Disclosure · 2006
Chilling effect

Argeniss planned to publish one Oracle database vulnerability a day for a week to pressure Oracle over slow patching, then cancelled amid rumors of financial or legal threats. disclose.io lists this as unconfirmed.

Corporate / private sector
Ambiguous / gray area (mixed or unclear intent)
Provocation
Disclosure series cancelled (pressure rumored)

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is a public 'bug a day' campaign legitimate pressure or reckless exposure of customers? (ii) Should frustration with a vendor's patch speed justify releasing unpatched flaws? (iii) Do rumored threats chill research as much as real ones?
